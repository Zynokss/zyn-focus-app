# Getting started

## Choose one thing

Open [Zyn Focus](https://focus.zynoks.com/). You can start immediately, or open Tasks to add a task. Tasks can have a project label, priority, estimated sessions, and a small next step. Select a task before starting if you want the session linked to it.

## Set your pace

Focus, Short break, and Long break are available above the dial. Settings contains durations, timing routines, long-break frequency, and automatic-start preferences. Change durations before starting a fresh session; changing a preference does not rewrite work already recorded.

Press **Start timer**. Pause and resume whenever needed. On the website, Space also starts or pauses while you are outside a form or dialog. The timer saves a deadline so a reload or a suspended tab can recover its state.

## Add quiet support

**Set target** lets you name what you want to finish. It is optional. **Got distracted** logs an interruption without pausing. **Stuck?** offers a smaller next step or a shorter stretch of work.

Open the sound controls, choose a mix, and press play. You can blend six original layers and set a sleep timer. Closing the drawer keeps playback running in the workspace. Navigating away from the workspace stops its audio.

The website has four background scenes, five accents, reduced motion, and an immersive view. Choose a comfortable setup in Settings.

## Break and reflect

After focus, use the break countdown and optional suggestions. A session review can record **Finished**, **Made progress**, or **Got stuck**, with a note and next step. Completing the linked task is a separate choice.

Focus tools includes your daily intention, minutes goal, completed-session history, project time, and weekly summary. The Later tab holds thoughts you want to return to and an optional 60-second breathing reset.

## End the day

The shutdown ritual saves what went well, loose ends, and tomorrow's first task or step. **Save & end day** stops audio and resets unfinished timing while keeping completed history and tasks.

Before relying on stored work, make a [backup](backup-and-restore.md). There is no cloud synchronization or account recovery.

## If you already have the Android preview

The app uses Focus, Tasks, Insights, and Space tabs. Space links to settings, routines, focus tools, weekly review, and Backup & restore. It stores its own data independently of the website. Android-specific alerts and widgets are preview features; device behavior still needs testing. The pictures in the main README show the website.
